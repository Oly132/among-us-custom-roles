AMONG US - IPAD BUILD CHECK (MAC)

This is a preparation/inspection tool, NOT the mod or an installer.
It does not change the game or iPad, download game files, sign into an account, or upload anything.

1. Unzip and open Among Us iPad Check.app on the Mac.
2. If Among Us is installed on that Mac, click Choose Among Us and select it from Applications.
3. Click Save report. Send the small Among-Us-iPad-build-report.json file back.

If the game is not installed or cannot be selected, Save report still gives the Mac processor/version information. Do not buy anything.

The app is locally/ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, opening this specific app may require System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other system protection globally.

No game binaries/assets, serial numbers, usernames, home-directory paths, account information or passwords are included in the report.

This version requires macOS 13 or later and includes both Intel and Apple Silicon binaries. Its parsing tests and compilation are checked in CI; real selection on your Mac is still needed.
